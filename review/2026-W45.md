## Item 3, Rusun Manggarai — A, B and C all REJECTED

This is the serious one. All three variants state the same wrong fact, and the
pipeline approved all three.

The verified fact the captions drew on reads:

> **claim:** Ada 1.500 peminat rusun Manggarai, 57 persen berusia 25-35 tahun
> dan belum menikah.
>
> **quote:** "Berdasarkan data peminatan yang dikumpulkan KAI Properti,
> rentang usia peminatnya 25-35 tahun dan sekitar 57 persen di antaranya belum
> menikah"

Read the quote. It says two separate things: the age range of the interested
buyers is 25 to 35, and about 57 percent **of them are unmarried**. The 57
percent attaches to being unmarried. It does not attach to the age range.

The extracted claim welds them together and says 57 percent are aged 25-35 and
unmarried. That is not what the article says, and all three captions repeat it:

- **A:** "Sekitar 57 persen peminatnya adalah usia 25-35 tahun seperti kamu."
- **B:** "Sekitar 57 persen peminatnya ditargetkan berusia 25-35 tahun dan
  belum menikah."
- **C:** "Menurut detikProperti, sekitar 57 persen peminat rumah susun ini
  berusia 25-35 tahun dan belum menikah."

B is worse than the other two. It also says the age range is "ditargetkan",
which turns a reported characteristic of the people who registered interest
into a target someone set.

### Root cause

The extractor writes two things per fact: a `claim` in its own words and a
`quote` copied from the article. Code verifies the quote, character for
character, as a substring of the fetched article. **Nothing verifies that the
claim is a fair reading of the quote.**

So the quote here is genuine and verified. The claim built on top of it is
wrong. The generator then wrote from the claim, and the claim check compared
the caption's numbers against the quote. 57 appears in the quote, so every
numeric check passed. What the 57 percent refers to is not a number, and
nothing in the pipeline constrains it.

### Approved edit for variant A

Replace:

> Sekitar 57 persen peminatnya adalah usia 25-35 tahun seperti kamu.

with:

> Peminatnya berusia 25-35 tahun, dan sekitar 57 persen di antaranya belum
> menikah.

With that one sentence replaced, variant A is approved.

### B and C

Rejected. Not edited. A carries the same information correctly once fixed, and
there is no reason to repair three captions about one project when only one
will be posted.

## Item 1, Survei BI — variant B, APPROVED WITH EDIT

The caption is accurate except for one sentence that merges two facts and
loses a qualifier:

> Suku bunga KPR tercatat relatif stabil di level 7,47 persen, dan mayoritas
> pembelian rumah dilakukan lewat KPR.

Two problems in one sentence. The two halves come from two different verified
facts, so no single quote supports the sentence as written. And the second
half drops "di pasar primer". The fact is about the primary market
specifically:

> "mayoritas pembelian rumah di pasar primer dilakukan melalui skema KPR
> dengan pangsa 70,68 persen"

Without that qualifier the caption claims that most home purchases in general
are made with a mortgage, which is a broader claim than the survey supports.

### Approved edit

Replace that sentence with two:

> Suku bunga KPR tercatat relatif stabil di level 7,47 persen. Mayoritas
> pembelian rumah di pasar primer dilakukan lewat KPR.

Approved with that edit.

## Item 1, Survei BI — variant C, APPROVED WITH EDIT

One sentence to fix:

> Mayoritas pembeli masih pakai KPR dengan pangsa 70,68 persen.

Two small errors. "Pembeli" is buyers; the fact is about "pembelian",
purchases. A share of purchases is not the same as a share of buyers. And
"masih" implies this is a continuation of something, which the fact does not
say. The qualifier "di pasar primer" is missing here too.

### Approved edit

> Mayoritas pembelian rumah di pasar primer dilakukan lewat KPR, dengan pangsa
> 70,68 persen.

Approved with that edit.

## Item 2, Rumah subsidi FLPP — variant B, APPROVED

Approved as generated. Every figure matches its quote, the source is named,
the hedge on the price ceiling is kept, and the voice is right.

One note, not a reason to reject. The caption describes FLPP as a route to a
first home and gives the rate and the price ceiling, but says nothing about
who is eligible. FLPP has income limits and other conditions, and a reader who
is over the income ceiling learns nothing here that would tell them so.

No rule in this pipeline can detect that, because it is an omission rather
than a false statement. Every check here asks whether what the caption says is
supported. None asks whether what it leaves out matters. If this caption is
posted, the eligibility conditions belong in the first comment or the graphic.

## What this review says about the pipeline

The gate approved all six captions. Reading them found three that state a
fact the article does not support, one scope error repeated in two variants,
and one omission that no check is designed to see.

The checks that exist work. They caught a great deal during the run itself,
and the figures in all six captions do match their quotes. The failures here
are all of the same kind: they are about meaning rather than about numbers,
and the code layer cannot reach meaning while the LLM layer was only ever
asked about support, not about accuracy of reading.

The 57 percent misread was not in the seeded-error set, and the claim checker
was never tested against it.
